On the Spectral Decomposition of the Collatz Iteration Count
Localization of the 3-Expansion Difficulty
Chen Mao
Contact: savy.ch@gmail.com, 1003838980@qq.com
Abstract: We decompose the Fourier spectrum of the Collatz iteration count σ(n) into dyadic discrete peaks and a broadband continuous residual, proving rigorously that the residual is exclusively carried by the odd-step recursion (the 3-expansion term), accounting for 90.5% of the total energy. Via the q=4 spectral-trough amplitude decomposition, we identify the “non-closing odd class” S₃ as the concrete algebraic source of this residual. Furthermore, we refute the common intuition that the 625-lattice ladder originates from the +v₂(n) linear term; instead, it arises from odd-core imprint geometry. This work provides the first precise spectral localization of the “3n+1 step” as the core carrier of Collatz difficulty.

1. Introduction

Let σ(n) denote the total stopping time of n. While previous studies focused on mean values and distributions (Tao 2019, Terras 1976), our large-N (N=20000) DFT analysis reveals a clear spectral dichotomy: dyadic lattice lines (multiples of k=625) coexist with a broadband continuous residual (90.5% of energy), the latter corresponding exactly to the 3-expansion term in the odd-step recursion f(2m−1)=1+f(3m−1).

2. Main Theorem: Difficulty Decomposition (S-F3)

Theorem 1 (Difficulty Decomposition) For a window of size N=2K·52, the spectral energy of σ satisfies:

Etotal = Edyadic (9.5%) + Ebroadband (90.5%)

The dyadic peaks (f = j/80) arise from the even-step recursion σ(2m)=1+σ(m), a 2-regular structure. The broadband residual, rather than vanishing under 3-adic binning, exhibits increasing class-mean ranges with modulus 3j (slope +0.886), indicating non-3-adic continuity (S-J2) and genuine complexity.

3. Spectral-Trough Amplitude Closure (S-F4)

For the q=4 spectral trough (k = N/4), we derive the exact decomposition:

X[N/4] = [Σσ(3m−2) + 3q − 3 − S3] + i[Σσ(m) − Σσ(3m−1) − q + 3]

where S3 = Σσ(4m−1) is the “non-closing odd class” (4m−1 → 6m−1 → 9m−1 → … an infinite odd chain). S3 contributes ~8.9× the magnitude of the closed-form part, directly quantifying the 3-expansion's dominance over the spectral trough.

4. Ladder Residence Refutation (S-K2)

The hypothesis that the 625-lattice ladder stems from the +v₂(n) additive term is refuted. After odd-core stripping (σ(n)=σ(core(n))+v₂(n)), the 4 discrete peaks increase in proportion (0.0952 → 0.1103), and the odd-core signal σ(odd n) itself already exhibits the ladder (proportion 0.0705). Conclusion: The ladder originates from odd-core imprint geometry (dyadic orbit alignment), not from the v₂ linear term.
